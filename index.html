What this is. A personal watch feed for vintage (original-era) Star Wars & Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles toys on Marktplaats. Modern reissues are filtered out. Items posted since the last sync are flagged NEW.
Seller read. Private find = a one-off / small private seller, where the underpriced buys tend to be. Dealer / Collector = an account running many varied listings. Flip on Opportunities only to hide the pros.
Market & scan. Market (eBay est.) is a rough loose/complete range for comparison — Onder markt means the ask sits below it. The scan line reports completeness (weapons/accessories); FOTO = read from the photo, LISTING = from the seller's text.
Estimates and classifications are guidance, not gospel. Prices and stock move fast — always confirm on Marktplaats. Not affiliated with Marktplaats or eBay.
